What the phone agent does

  • Answers the calls you set up, including outside opening hours.
  • Tells the caller at the start that they are speaking to an AI system.
  • Notes who called, about which vehicle and what they want.
  • Puts a summary in the file after every call, with the next step included.
  • Makes no price deal and names no trade-in figure on the phone.
How it works

From call to follow-up. In six steps.

  1. 01A call comes in
  2. 02The phone agent picks up
  3. 03The conversation is handled
  4. 04Name, vehicle and question are noted
  5. 05A summary is in the file
  6. 06Your salesperson follows up
