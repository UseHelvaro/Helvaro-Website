Home · Agents · Damage intake
Agent 08 of ten

With damage, the work starts by asking questions.

Before you can say anything about repair or cost, you need to know what happened, who is liable and which insurer is involved. Collecting that information takes half an hour on the phone, and usually happens at the busiest moment of the day.

Book a demo → This is how the system fits together
Without this

A customer who has just had an accident does not know what to do either.

Someone calls after a collision. He is shaken, does not know whether to call the insurer or the garage first, and has no idea which details are needed. Your front desk becomes first aid, and that costs time you do not have.

What is needed is the same every time: registration, what happened, photos of the damage, the insurer, the policy number and whether there is another party. Without those six you can plan nothing and estimate nothing.

A customer who is helped well on the first day almost always ends up at that garage for the repair.

  • The car is there without anyone knowing who is paying.
  • The estimate waits on photos that are not there.
  • A customer kept waiting drives on to a bodyshop chain.
The workflow

What the system does.

The six things you need, collected before the car drives in.

  1. 01

    Calm first, then data

    The first question is whether everyone is unhurt and whether the car still drives. That decides whether a recovery truck is needed or an appointment will do.

  2. 02

    Registration and vehicle

    The registration provides make, model and year. That is the basis for every estimate that follows.

  3. 03

    Photos in the conversation

    The customer sends photos of the damage, the whole vehicle and the mileage. Over WhatsApp that is, for most people, the easiest step in the entire process.

  4. 04

    Insurance and liability

    Which insurer, which policy number, who was liable and has an accident form been filled in. Exactly the questions a customer does not think of himself.

  5. 05

    An appointment or a referral

    If the car still drives, a moment is booked to look at it. If it does not, it goes straight to a person.

  6. 06

    The file is ready

    When the car comes in, everything is already there. You do not have to start asking questions again.

Customer82-ABC-3 · photos sent

File complete Thursday · 10:00 · damage assessment · photos and policy received
What it produces

The first day decides where the car gets repaired.

You are there while it happens

Whoever responds within ten minutes after a collision is the garage that customer goes to. In the evenings and at weekends too.

No estimate without photos

The images come in at the moment the customer is already dealing with it, not three days later.

Less digging at the front desk

The half hour of asking questions is already done before the car pulls up.

A complete file

Insurer, policy, liability and images are all together. That saves hassle with the assessor.

Limits

What this agent does not do.

  • He does not produce an estimate and names no repair amount.
  • He does not judge liability and makes no statement about cover.
  • He does not report the damage to an insurer.
  • With injuries or a car that no longer drives, it goes straight to a person.
