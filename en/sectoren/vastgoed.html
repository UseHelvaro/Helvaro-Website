The problem is timing, not your team

A listing gets most of its responses outside office hours. Saturday evening, Sunday morning, during a viewing of another property. At that moment the same prospect is comparing your offer with three other agencies. Calling back on Monday morning is often too late.

What your agent does

  1. Answering within thirty secondsEvery response to a listing or form gets a personal message straight away, in the evening and at weekends too.
  2. QualifyingIs the prospect buying to live in or to invest, how many bedrooms, what budget, how urgent. Buyer or seller becomes clear by itself.
  3. Booking the viewingOnly those who fit get a free slot from the right agent's calendar. Confirmation appears straight away in the chat.
  4. Following up after the visitAfter the viewing your agent asks what the prospect thought. That way no file goes quiet.
  5. Spotting sellersSomeone asking what their home is worth is not a buyer. They go into a different conversation, aimed at a valuation appointment.

Extra for real estate. The visualisation agent creates images of a property in different architectural styles. A prospect sees what is possible with an unrenovated home before a single brick is moved.
