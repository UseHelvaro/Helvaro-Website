Only quotes worth your time.

Drawing up a quote costs half a day. Answering a price shopper costs the same half day. Helvaro asks the questions first, so your team only works on requests that lead somewhere.

Book a work audit →

Two kinds of loss

The first is visible: hours spent on requests from people who only wanted a ballpark figure. The second is quieter and more expensive: quotes that went out and that nobody ever chased. At most companies that second pile is bigger than the first.

What your agent does

  1. Asking before you draw anything upProject type, timing, location and budget. Anyone who will not answer those was never going to become a customer.
  2. Sorting on feasibilityDoes the request fit your region, your schedule and the kind of work you do. What does not fit gets a polite answer instead of a quote.
  3. Booking a site visitSerious requests get a free slot in the right project manager's calendar straight away.
  4. Chasing quotesA week after sending, your agent asks how things stand. Not once, but until there is an answer or the customer clearly drops out.
  5. Re-offering old quotesA quote from eight months ago is not a lost cause. Often it was a postponement, not a cancellation.

Extra for renovation. The visualisation agent creates images of a property in different architectural styles. A hesitating client sees what is possible before a quote is even on the table.
