The system

Capture. Qualify. Follow up.

What happens after the first question decides where the buyer signs.

01

Capture

Every question on your website and WhatsApp gets an answer straight away, with price, mileage and trim from your own stock.

02

Qualifying

Budget, timing, trade-in and financing land in one file. What the buyer does not say is not filled in.

03

Follow up

The appointment is in your Google Calendar. If a buyer goes quiet, they get three follow-ups and then no more.
