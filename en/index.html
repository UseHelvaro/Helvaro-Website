The problem

Your workshop does not have a lead problem. You have a follow-up problem.

The demand is already coming in. What happens next decides whether there is a car on the ramp.

01

The phone rings while everyone is busy

The front desk is helping a customer, the mechanics are on a car. The caller waits through three rings and then calls the garage down the road. You do not even know he called.

Gone without a trace.

02

The quote has been sent and then it goes quiet

A price for a clutch or a timing belt goes out. There is no yes and no no. Nobody follows up, because there is always something more urgent.

Half the work, all the cost.

03

The MOT is approaching and you wait

You know exactly which cars are due this month. The customer no longer remembers, and books with whoever messages him first.

A regular customer drifting away.

04

A no-show leaves a gap in the planning

Someone does not turn up. The slot is too late to fill, so for an hour there is nothing on the ramp while the costs carry on.

An empty ramp costs as much as a full one.

05

Last year's customer does not come back

No argument, no complaint. He has simply forgotten you. There are hundreds of those names in your system, and nobody has the time to call them.

A customer base that slowly empties out.

06

Outside opening hours nothing happens

A customer who has broken down reads your website in the evening and sends a message. At half past eight tomorrow morning it is sitting in an inbox. By then he has already found someone.

Half the week does not count.

None of these six is about having too few customers. They are all about customers you already had.

Control

Your team decides. Your agents do the rest.

Agents acting on their own without you seeing what they do is precisely what we do not build. Six commitments, fixed before we start.

01

Every conversation readable

You read every conversation live and in full afterwards. Export whenever you want. A summary is never the only thing left.

02

Approval per action type

You decide which actions an agent may carry out alone and which go past a person first. Per type, not one switch for everything.

03

Escalation with context

If an agent cannot resolve it, it hands over to a named person at your end, with the whole conversation and the reason attached. Within an agreed time.

04

Action log

What the agent did, on which source, when, and who approved it. Exportable, including when your insurer or auditor asks for it.

05

Data in the EU

Processing within the EU, a published sub-processor list, a standard data processing agreement and a retention period you set yourself.

06

Emergency stop

You stop any agent yourself, immediately, without calling us. Per agent, not everything at once.

What we do not claim: that an agent never makes a mistake. That is exactly why the approvals above exist. Our model providers process approved context under their own terms, and we put that in writing before we start.
